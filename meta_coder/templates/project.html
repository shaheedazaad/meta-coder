CSV format and template
  • row_id — effect ID: a short, unique identifier you invent for this row.
  • source_pdf — which uploaded PDF this effect comes from (exact filename).
  • locator — effect location, e.g. "Table 2, Experiment 1, DV: accuracy".
  • authors — paper identification.
  • year — paper identification.
